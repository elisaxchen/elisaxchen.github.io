I spent enough time volunteering in bike shops to call myself a bike mechanic in a previous life, but now I enjoy building tech products that (1) connect people better socially (I study social cohesion, as you can see!) or (2) support missions and social values I believe in. I love elegant design and I'm quite obsessed with it.

<div class="project-item">
<h3><span class="project-date">Oct 2024</span><span class="project-dot">·</span><a href="https://devpost.com/software/herhealth-women-s-empowerment-and-health-education">WhispHER</a></h3>
<div class="project-subtitle">A Confidential AI Chatbot for Women's Health in Developing Contexts</div>
<p class="project-context">My brilliant friend <a href="https://github.com/DoraSzasz">Teodora Szasz</a> and I built this for the Google She Builds AI Hackathon, which challenged participants to develop AI-powered solutions addressing gender equity as outlined in UN Sustainable Development Goal 5.</p>
<p>Millions of women lack access to vital health information due to societal norms. WhispHER is a Gemini-powered chatbot accessible through WhatsApp that provides a confidential, judgment-free space for young women to ask questions and receive scientifically backed answers about sexual health, menstrual care, and overall well-being. By offering reliable information anonymously, WhispHER is designed to break through cultural taboos that have long kept girls in the dark.</p>
</div>

<div class="project-item">
<h3><span class="project-date">Sep 2022</span><span class="project-dot">·</span><a href="https://github.com/miielab/miienlp">MiiE NLP</a></h3>
<div class="project-subtitle">I led the development of this open-source package with the team at the MiiE Lab, Harris School of Public Policy, UChicago.</div>
<p>MiiE NLP is a one-stop shop for social scientists to use natural language processing techniques to analyze text. We built it to make NLP and computational content analysis accessible to researchers with zero or limited Python experience, and we hope it helps catalyze new research in the social and computational social sciences.</p>
</div>
